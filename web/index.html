§04 — Method

The seven tells

What the English → Claudish spec reaches for. Meaning is preserved; only the framing moves.

  1. Contrastive reframing

    Recast a plain fact as X, not Y — a contrast the sentence already supported, now made load-bearing.

  2. Structural framing

    Describe relationships as boundaries, layers, seams, gates, handoffs and surfaces. Everything becomes a system with parts.

  3. Process framing

    Reach for cadence, drift, wedged and provenance — but only where the input genuinely supports it.

  4. Compression into abstractions

    An ordinary relationship gets a compact technical name. Only owners can merge becomes merge authority is restricted to the owner role.

  5. Parallelism

    Balanced clauses, mirrored syntax, semicolons. One idea, delivered twice, symmetrically.

  6. Hyphenated compression

    Dense noun-as-adjective stacks: owner-gated, quality-gated, approval-backed, routing-layer. Both halves have to come from the input.

  7. Claude-like cadence

    A polished explanatory clause, then a shorter, sharper one. That is the shape.
